How ATS Scan AI handles information

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Information site

This learning website provides public product and educational content. It does not itself provide resume analysis or payment processing.

Main application data

When you use the main ATS Scan AI application, information may include your email address, account profile, uploaded resume files, extracted resume text, job descriptions or role selections, scan results, credit transactions, and payment references.

Purpose of processing

Information is used to authenticate users, provide resume analysis, store limited scan history, manage credits, verify payments, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and provide support.

Service providers

The product may rely on hosting, database, storage, email delivery, AI-processing, payment, DNS, security, and monitoring providers. These providers process information according to their service terms and applicable agreements.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. ATS Scan AI receives payment identifiers and status information needed to verify the transaction and add credits. Card or bank credentials are entered in the payment provider's checkout.

Retention

The application maintains a limited scan history and may remove older scans and associated stored resume files. Operational, payment, and security records may be kept for longer where reasonably necessary.

Your choices

You may contact support to ask questions about your information or request appropriate account assistance. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or transaction purposes.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Email privacy or account questions to support@atsscanai.com.
