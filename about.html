About ATS Scan AI

Practical AI guidance for better job applications

ATS Scan AI was created to help job seekers understand resume-to-role alignment before submitting an application.

Our focus

Resume advice is often vague. ATS Scan AI focuses on specific signals such as skills, keywords, formatting, measurable achievements, readability, and role relevance.

Responsible use

The application is a guidance tool. Users should verify all suggestions, keep every claim accurate, and adapt recommendations to their real experience and target role.

Product access

The information website is hosted at learn.atsscanai.com. Resume uploads, authentication, reports, credits, and payments are handled in the main application at atsscanai.com.
