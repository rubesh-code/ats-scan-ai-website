About ATS Scan AI

Practical resume feedback for more informed job applications

ATS Scan AI helps job seekers review resume relevance, clarity, keywords, skills, and formatting before submitting an application.

Our purpose

Resume advice is often vague, while job descriptions are highly specific. ATS Scan AI is designed to connect the two by turning a resume and role context into a structured, understandable report.

The service is not intended to replace professional judgment. Instead, it gives users a repeatable way to identify gaps, improve truthful presentation, and compare revisions.

What we focus on

Clarity

Present report sections in straightforward language so users know what to review next.

Relevance

Use job-role context to identify skills and keywords that matter for the intended application.

Responsible use

Encourage users to verify AI suggestions and keep every resume statement accurate.
