How it works

Get an ATS report in three focused steps

The analysis combines your resume, job context, and previous scans to produce actionable feedback.

1

Upload the resume

Choose a PDF, DOCX, or TXT resume. The application extracts the document text so it can evaluate structure and content.

2

Add job context

Paste a target job description, choose a supported role, or provide a custom role. More specific context generally produces more relevant feedback.

3

Generate and review the report

Review the ATS score, matched skills, missing skills, missing keywords, formatting issues, stronger bullet-point ideas, and final recommendation.

How previous scans are used

Previous results may be used to highlight progress, repeated issues, new gaps, and score changes. The current ATS score should still be based on the current resume and current role context.

Important

AI-generated recommendations can contain errors or unsuitable wording. Review every suggestion, keep claims truthful, and adjust the language to match your real experience.
