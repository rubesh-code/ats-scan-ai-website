What is an ATS resume checker?

An applicant tracking system, commonly called an ATS, helps employers collect, organize, search, and review applications. An ATS resume checker evaluates whether your resume presents information in a clear, relevant, and machine-readable way.

No single score can reproduce every employer’s system. ATS Scan AI therefore treats the score as a practical comparison tool: use it to find gaps, revise the resume, and measure whether the next version is more aligned.

What ATS Scan AI checks

Role relevance

The analysis uses the job description, selected role, or custom role you provide as context.

Skills and keywords

It identifies matched skills, missing skills, and missing role-related keywords.

Resume structure

It highlights formatting issues that may reduce readability or make information harder to extract.

Experience and education

It reviews whether the resume clearly communicates relevant experience and educational background.

Better bullet points

It suggests ways to make achievements and responsibilities clearer and more specific.

Final recommendation

It summarizes the most important next actions for improving the document.

How to use your ATS score

Compare the score with the report details rather than focusing only on the number. A useful revision should improve accuracy, clarity, and role relevance without inserting keywords that do not truthfully describe your background.

Important: ATS Scan AI provides AI-assisted guidance. It does not represent any employer, reproduce every ATS product, or guarantee an interview, ranking, or job offer.

Practical ways to improve a resume

  • Use standard headings such as Experience, Education, Skills, and Projects.
  • Match relevant terminology from the job description only when it accurately describes your experience.
  • Use measurable achievements instead of vague responsibility statements.
  • Keep dates, job titles, employers, and education details easy to identify.
  • Avoid complex layouts that make the reading order unclear.
  • Proofread every AI suggestion before adding it to your resume.
