What the checker reviews

ATS Scan AI analyzes the resume file together with a job description or selected role. The report can highlight keyword alignment, skills, formatting observations, and areas where evidence may be weak.

How to use the score

Treat the score as a diagnostic signal rather than a hiring prediction. Review the details behind it: missing requirements, weak evidence, unclear wording, and formatting issues.

What to improve first

  1. Confirm the resume is readable and text-based.
  2. Match the role's important skills and responsibilities truthfully.
  3. Show evidence through outcomes, scale, speed, quality, or ownership.
  4. Remove generic statements that do not help the target role.

Avoid keyword stuffing

Do not paste every term from the job description. Add a keyword only where your experience genuinely supports it, then explain how you used that skill.
