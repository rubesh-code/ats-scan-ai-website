How it works

A focused resume review in three steps

Provide the resume and role context, then use the report to guide practical revisions.

1

Upload your resume

Choose a supported PDF, DOCX, or TXT file. A clean text-based export gives the analyzer better input.

2

Add the role context

Paste a job description or select a role. This helps the analysis focus on the requirements that matter for that application.

3

Review and improve

Use the score, keyword gaps, formatting observations, and suggestions to revise the resume truthfully.
