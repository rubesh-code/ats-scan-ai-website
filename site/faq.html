ATS Scan AI questions and answers

Clear information about scores, files, keywords, pricing, and responsible use.

What is an ATS resume score?

It is a diagnostic score based on the resume and the role context provided. It should not be treated as a guarantee of selection.

Which resume files are supported?

The application supports PDF, DOCX, and TXT files within the displayed file-size limit.

Should I copy every keyword from the job description?

No. Add terms only where your genuine experience supports them, and show evidence through projects, responsibilities, or outcomes.

Is PDF or DOCX better for ATS?

Follow the employer instruction first. In either format, confirm the resume contains selectable text and uses a simple structure.

How many analyses are included in ₹99?

The current package provides 10 credits, with one credit used per completed resume analysis.

Does a high score guarantee an interview?

No. Hiring depends on experience, evidence, competition, recruiter judgment, and other factors beyond an automated review.
