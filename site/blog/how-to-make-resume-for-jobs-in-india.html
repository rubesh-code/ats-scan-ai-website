How to Make a Resume for Jobs in India in 2026: ATS-Friendly Guide + Examples

A practical guide for freshers, students, career changers and experienced professionals applying for jobs in India.

ATS Scan AI Editorial Team Updated 7 August 2026 14 min read
Quick answer

For most jobs in India, use a clean resume with standard headings, a short role-specific summary, relevant skills, evidence from experience or projects, and simple ATS-readable formatting.

Creating a resume for jobs in India is no longer just about listing your education, skills and previous companies. Recruiters may receive hundreds of applications for one opening, while many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems to organise and review applications.

Whether you are a fresher looking for your first job, an experienced professional planning a switch, or a student applying for internships, your resume needs to do two things well: it should be easy for software to understand and easy for a recruiter to scan.

What is a resume?

A resume is a concise professional document explaining why your background is relevant to a particular job.

It normally contains:

  • Contact information
  • Professional summary
  • Skills
  • Work experience
  • Internships and projects
  • Education
  • Relevant certifications
  • Achievements

The purpose is not to describe your entire life. The goal is to earn the interview.

CV vs resume: what is the difference in India?

In everyday Indian recruitment, the words CV and resume are sometimes used interchangeably. Technically, however, a resume is usually shorter and customised for a particular job, while a CV can be more detailed and is common in academic, scientific, medical and research settings.

For most private-sector roles in software, finance, marketing, sales, operations, customer support and similar fields, a focused resume is normally the better approach.

Best resume format for jobs in India

For most candidates, a simple reverse-chronological structure works well.

1. Name and contact details

Rahul Sharma
Bengaluru, Karnataka
Phone number • Professional email • LinkedIn • Portfolio or GitHub

You generally do not need to add highly personal information such as:

  • Father's or mother's name
  • Religion
  • Marital status
  • Aadhaar number
  • Passport number
  • Full residential address

2. Add a strong resume summary

A good resume summary is normally two to four lines covering your strongest qualifications, relevant skills and target role.

Fresher resume summary example

Computer Science graduate with hands-on experience in Java, Spring Boot, React and MySQL through academic and personal projects. Built full-stack applications involving REST APIs, authentication and database integration. Seeking an entry-level software developer role where I can apply my development and problem-solving skills.

Avoid generic summaries such as “I am a hardworking and passionate individual looking for an opportunity to grow.” Your summary should explain what makes you relevant to the target role.

3. Add skills relevant to the job

For a Java developer role, for example:

Programming: Java, JavaScript, SQL
Backend: Spring Boot, REST APIs
Frontend: React, HTML, CSS
Database: MySQL, PostgreSQL
Tools: Git, GitHub, Postman

Do not fill the section with every technology you have ever encountered. Include skills you can genuinely explain during an interview.

4. Match your resume to the job description

If the job repeatedly mentions React, JavaScript, REST APIs, Git and responsive web development, make sure your resume clearly shows where you have actually used those skills.

Weak: Worked on frontend development.
Better: Built responsive React interfaces and integrated REST APIs using JavaScript while managing source code through Git.

Do not copy the entire job description. Use relevant terminology naturally where your real experience supports it.

5. How freshers should write experience

If you have no full-time work experience, use evidence from:

  • Internships
  • College projects
  • Personal projects
  • Freelance work
  • Hackathons
  • Open-source contributions
  • Research
  • Relevant certifications
Weak project bullet: Created an e-commerce website using React.
Better: Developed a React-based e-commerce application with product search, shopping cart functionality and REST API integration.

6. How experienced candidates should write work experience

Focus on actions, responsibilities and outcomes rather than copying a job description.

Weak: Responsible for handling customers.
Better: Managed 40+ customer requests weekly and coordinated with technical teams to resolve escalated service issues.

A useful formula is action + responsibility + result.

7. How to make your resume stand out

Standing out does not require an unusual design. Strong information is often more valuable than decorative graphics.

Use measurable achievements

Increased monthly sales by 18% through targeted follow-ups with existing customers.

Show what you actually built

Built three responsive React projects using reusable components and API integration.

Avoid filling valuable space with generic phrases such as hardworking, punctual, honest, dynamic or dedicated unless your experience demonstrates those qualities.

8. Make the resume ATS-friendly

Use:

  • Standard section headings
  • Simple readable fonts
  • Clear sections
  • Normal bullet points
  • Relevant job keywords
  • Consistent dates
  • Text-based PDF or DOCX files

Be careful with:

  • Complicated multiple-column layouts
  • Charts and skill-rating bars
  • Important text embedded in images
  • Decorative icons carrying essential information
  • Complex tables
  • Excessive graphics

9. Should your resume be one page?

Freshers

For many freshers, one page is enough. Prioritise relevant projects, internships, education and skills.

Experienced candidates

One or two pages may be appropriate depending on experience. Do not shrink the font to an unreadable size simply to force everything onto one page.

11. Should you create one resume and apply everywhere?

No. Keep one master resume and adjust the most important sections for serious applications.

Usually customise:

  • Professional summary
  • Skills
  • Relevant projects
  • Work-experience bullets
  • Job-related terminology

12. Where to apply for jobs in India

Job seekers commonly use a combination of:

  • LinkedIn
  • Naukri
  • Indeed
  • Internshala
  • Company career pages
  • College placement portals
  • Referrals
  • Professional communities

Do not depend entirely on application volume. Better role targeting and a relevant resume can be more useful than repeatedly sending the same document.

13. Check your resume before applying

Before sending another batch of applications, check whether your resume actually matches the job you are targeting.

A resume checker can help highlight:

  • Missing role-specific keywords
  • Skills alignment
  • Resume structure
  • ATS readability
  • Weak or incomplete sections
  • Job-description matching

Final resume checklist for jobs in India

  • Your name and contact information are correct.
  • Your email address looks professional.
  • The resume matches the job you are applying for.
  • Important job skills appear naturally where relevant.
  • Your summary is specific rather than generic.
  • Projects explain what you actually built.
  • Experience bullets focus on contributions and results.
  • The formatting is simple and readable.
  • Spelling and grammar have been checked.
  • Your LinkedIn and portfolio links work.
  • The file name is professional, such as Rahul-Sharma-Resume.pdf.
  • You have not added skills you cannot explain in an interview.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a resume for a job in India?

Start with contact information, add a concise professional summary, relevant skills, experience or projects, education and certifications. Customise the strongest sections according to the job description.

What is the best resume format for freshers in India?

A clean one-page reverse-chronological resume is a strong starting point. Focus on skills, projects, internships, education and evidence relevant to the target role.

Is a CV and resume the same in India?

The terms are often used interchangeably, but a resume is normally shorter and job-focused while a CV may contain more detailed academic or professional information.

Should I put a photo on my resume in India?

For most corporate and technology roles, a photo is unnecessary unless the employer specifically requests one.

PDF or Word: which is better?

Both can work. Follow the employer's requested format and ensure the file contains selectable text and maintains a simple structure.

Can freshers make an ATS-friendly resume?

Yes. Freshers can use clear headings, relevant skills, internships, projects and truthful keywords that match the target job.

Conclusion

A successful resume for jobs in India does not need an expensive template or complicated design. It needs relevance.

Understand the position, identify the skills the employer needs and clearly demonstrate where you have used those skills. Freshers can use projects and internships as evidence, while experienced candidates should focus on responsibilities, achievements and measurable outcomes.

Instead of sending the exact same resume everywhere, customise the strongest parts for each serious application. That makes your resume more useful to recruitment software, recruiters and hiring managers.
