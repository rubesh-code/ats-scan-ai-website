ATS Resume Format for Freshers in India (2026): Template, Keywords & Examples

Build a clean one-page fresher resume that is easy for applicant tracking systems to parse and clear for recruiters to review.

ATS Scan AI Editorial TeamUpdated 5 August 202614 min read
Quick answer

The best ATS resume format for most freshers in India is a one-page, single-column resume with standard headings, a role-specific summary, relevant skills, education, projects, internships, certifications, and measurable achievements. Use a text-based PDF or DOCX, avoid tables and graphics, and tailor the wording to each job description.

Why resume format matters for freshers

A fresher usually has less full-time experience than an experienced candidate, so structure becomes especially important. Your resume must help both software and human reviewers quickly identify your target role, education, technical or functional skills, practical work, and evidence of contribution.

An applicant tracking system may extract information such as your name, contact details, education, employers, dates, skills, and job titles. Complicated designs can interrupt that extraction. A visually impressive document is not useful if important information is placed inside a graphic, text box, header, footer, or multi-column layout that is parsed incorrectly.

The purpose of an ATS-friendly format is not to make every resume look identical. It is to create a reliable structure that preserves your information while allowing your projects and achievements to stand out.

Recommended ATS resume section order for freshers

Use the following order as a strong default. You may move projects above education when your practical work is more relevant to the job than your degree title.

  1. Name and contact information
  2. Professional summary
  3. Skills
  4. Education
  5. Projects
  6. Internships or practical experience
  7. Certifications
  8. Achievements, leadership, or volunteering

Use standard headings such as Summary, Skills, Education, Projects, and Experience. Creative headings such as “My Journey” or “What I Bring” may be understandable to a person but less predictable for resume parsers.

1. Write a simple resume header

Place your contact details in the main document body, not inside the Word header or footer. Include:

  • Full name
  • Professional email address
  • Phone number with country code when applying internationally
  • City and state
  • LinkedIn profile, portfolio, or GitHub link when relevant
Example

Ananya Rao
Bengaluru, Karnataka · +91 98XXXXXX10 · ananya.rao@email.com
linkedin.com/in/ananyarao · github.com/ananyarao

Unless the employer specifically asks for it, avoid a photograph, date of birth, marital status, religion, gender, Aadhaar number, passport number, or full street address. These details do not demonstrate job fit and may add unnecessary privacy risk.

2. Use a role-specific professional summary

A fresher summary should be two or three lines. Name the target role, strongest relevant skills, and one piece of evidence. Avoid generic objectives such as “looking for a challenging position in a growing company.”

Weak fresher summary

Hardworking graduate seeking a challenging opportunity where I can learn and grow while contributing to the company.

Stronger software developer summary

Computer Science graduate targeting junior React developer roles, with hands-on experience in React, TypeScript, REST APIs, Git, and responsive UI development through three deployed projects.

Stronger data analyst summary

Entry-level data analyst with practical experience in SQL, Excel, Power BI, Python, and dashboard development, including a sales-analysis project covering more than 50,000 transaction records.

The stronger versions are specific, searchable, and supported by evidence that can be verified in the Projects section.

3. Build a focused skills section

Do not paste every tool you have ever used. Select skills that appear in the job description and that you can discuss confidently in an interview.

Group related skills so the section remains readable:

  • Programming: Java, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript
  • Frontend: React, HTML, CSS, Tailwind CSS
  • Data: SQL, Excel, Power BI, Pandas
  • Tools: Git, GitHub, Postman, Jira
  • Methods: REST API integration, responsive design, data cleaning

Important skills should also appear naturally in your project or internship bullets. A standalone keyword list is weaker than evidence showing how you used those skills.

4. Present education clearly

For freshers, education is usually important. List the most recent qualification first. Include the institution, degree, specialization, location, and graduation year. Add CGPA or percentage when it strengthens your application or when the employer requests it.

Example

Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering
ABC Institute of Technology, Chennai · 2022–2026
CGPA: 8.4/10

Relevant coursework may help when the role requires subjects that are not obvious from the degree title. Keep the list short and role-specific.

5. Treat projects as evidence, not decoration

Projects are often the strongest section in a fresher resume. Each project should explain:

  • The problem or user need
  • The tools or methods used
  • Your specific contribution
  • The measurable result, scale, or improvement

Weak project bullet

Created an e-commerce website using React and Node.js.

Stronger project bullets

  • Built a responsive e-commerce interface using React and TypeScript, including product filtering, cart management, and reusable components.
  • Integrated REST APIs for 500+ product records and reduced repeated network requests by adding client-side caching.
  • Added form validation and tested checkout workflows across mobile and desktop screen sizes.

Use numbers only when they are accurate. Useful measurements include dataset size, number of users, pages, API endpoints, test cases, response time, accuracy, team size, delivery period, or percentage improvement.

6. Write internship and practical-experience bullets

Internships, freelance assignments, college clubs, volunteering, hackathons, and family-business responsibilities can demonstrate real work. Focus on actions and outcomes rather than simply listing duties.

Before

Responsible for social media and reports.

After

Prepared weekly performance reports in Excel and scheduled social content across three channels, helping the team identify the highest-engagement campaign topics.

Start bullets with active verbs such as built, analyzed, automated, designed, implemented, tested, coordinated, or improved.

7. Add job-description keywords truthfully

Keywords are not secret words that guarantee selection. They are the skills, tools, qualifications, methods, and responsibilities the employer considers important.

Use this process for each application:

  1. Highlight repeated technical skills, tools, certifications, and role terms in the job description.
  2. Separate required qualifications from preferred qualifications.
  3. Identify which terms accurately match your projects, education, internship, or skills.
  4. Add those terms in context rather than repeating them unnaturally.
  5. Leave out skills you do not possess.

For example, when a job description requests React, TypeScript, REST API integration, Git, responsive design, and unit testing, the resume should show where those skills were used. Writing “React” five times without evidence is less useful than one project bullet explaining what you built with it.

For a step-by-step keyword method, read How to Find Resume Keywords in a Job Description.

8. Follow ATS-friendly formatting rules

  • Use a single-column layout.
  • Use a readable font such as Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Georgia, or Times New Roman.
  • Keep body text around 10–12 points.
  • Use margins of at least 0.5 inch.
  • Use consistent dates, spacing, headings, and bullet styles.
  • Use reverse chronological order within education and experience sections.
  • Keep the resume to one page when the strongest relevant evidence fits comfortably.
  • Avoid tables, text boxes, charts, icons, skill bars, logos, and decorative graphics.
  • Do not place important information in headers or footers.
  • Do not upload an image or scanned copy of the resume.

Simple formatting is not the same as poor design. Good spacing, bold headings, consistent typography, and concise bullets can create a professional document without relying on complex visual elements.

9. Choose PDF or DOCX carefully

Follow the application instructions first. When the employer accepts both formats, use:

  • PDF when you want to preserve layout and have confirmed that the text is selectable.
  • DOCX when the employer or portal specifically prefers an editable document.

Do not submit an image-only PDF. Open the file after exporting and verify that you can select and copy the text. Many recruiting platforms support common formats such as DOC, DOCX, PDF, RTF, and TXT, but individual employer portals may apply their own restrictions.

For a detailed comparison, read PDF vs DOCX for ATS.

10 common fresher resume mistakes to avoid

  1. Using the same generic resume for every application
  2. Writing a vague career objective
  3. Listing skills without project or internship evidence
  4. Adding unsupported keywords
  5. Using multi-column templates, tables, and graphics
  6. Putting contact details inside a header or footer
  7. Describing projects in one vague sentence
  8. Using passive phrases such as “responsible for” throughout the resume
  9. Including irrelevant personal information
  10. Submitting without checking spelling, grammar, links, and file readability

One-page ATS resume template for freshers

Copy this section order

FULL NAME
City, State · Phone · Email · LinkedIn · Portfolio/GitHub

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
Two or three lines naming the target role, strongest relevant skills, and practical evidence.

SKILLS
Grouped technical, functional, language, platform, or tool skills relevant to the role.

EDUCATION
Degree · Specialization · Institution · Graduation year · CGPA/percentage when relevant.

PROJECTS
Project name · Tools · 2–4 evidence-based bullets · Link when useful.

INTERNSHIPS / EXPERIENCE
Organization · Role · Dates · Action-and-result bullets.

CERTIFICATIONS / ACHIEVEMENTS
Only relevant and verifiable items.

Final ATS resume checklist for Indian freshers

  • The target role is clear within the first few lines.
  • The resume uses a single-column layout and standard headings.
  • Contact details appear in the main body.
  • Important job-description keywords are used accurately.
  • Projects explain tools, contribution, and outcome.
  • Skills are supported by evidence elsewhere in the resume.
  • Dates and formatting are consistent.
  • There are no photos, skill bars, tables, or text boxes.
  • The PDF text can be selected and copied.
  • The file name is professional, such as Ananya_Rao_React_Developer_Resume.pdf.
  • Every statement is truthful and can be explained in an interview.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ATS resume format for freshers in India?

A clean one-page, single-column resume with standard headings, reverse-chronological education and experience, relevant skills, and evidence-based projects is a strong starting point for most freshers.

Should a fresher include a photo?

Usually no. Unless the employer specifically requests one, a photo does not demonstrate job fit and may create parsing or privacy concerns.

Is PDF or DOCX better?

Follow the employer's instructions. A text-based PDF or DOCX is generally suitable, but avoid scanned files and complicated layouts.

Can a fresher resume be two pages?

Most freshers can use one page. Use a second page only when you have substantial relevant projects, internships, research, certifications, or leadership experience that genuinely supports the target role.

How should I add keywords?

Use relevant terms from the job description in your summary, skills, projects, and experience only where they truthfully describe your work or knowledge.

Does a high ATS score guarantee an interview?

No. Use the score to identify relevance, keyword, formatting, and evidence gaps. Recruiter judgment and competition still determine the outcome.
