UGC NET June 2026 Answer Key: NTA Release Timeline & Objection Process

The National Testing Agency is set to release the provisional answer keys for UGC-NET June 2026 and Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026. Here is what candidates should know about the release timeline, objections, final answer keys and results.

Published: August 10, 2026 · Updated: August 10, 2026

Candidates waiting for the UGC-NET June 2026 answer key have received an important update from the National Testing Agency (NTA). According to the latest notice reported on August 10, 2026, the provisional answer key for UGC-NET June 2026 is expected to be released on the official NTA website this week.

NTA is also set to publish provisional answer keys for the Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 examination, along with the ICAR AIEEA (PG) and AICE JRF/SRF (PhD) 2026 examinations.

Quick update

  • UGC-NET June 2026 provisional answer key is expected this week.
  • Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 provisional answer key is also expected.
  • Candidates will get an opportunity to challenge answers during the notified objection window.
  • Objections should include valid justification and supporting evidence.
  • Final answer keys and results will follow after the objection process is completed and reviewed.

When will the UGC NET June 2026 answer key be released?

The latest announcement says that NTA is set to release the provisional UGC-NET June 2026 answer key this week. The same update covers the provisional answer key for the Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 examination.

Candidates should rely on the official NTA website and official notifications for the exact publication time and objection-window dates rather than relying on unofficial social-media updates.

When was UGC-NET June 2026 conducted?

According to the latest report, the UGC-NET June 2026 examination was conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode from June 22 to June 30, 2026. A retest was subsequently conducted on July 5, 2026.

When was the CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 exam conducted?

The Joint CSIR-UGC NET June 2026 examination was held on July 17 and July 18, 2026, according to the reported NTA timeline.

What should candidates do after the provisional answer key is released?

Once the provisional answer key becomes available, candidates should compare it carefully with their recorded responses. Do not immediately assume that an answer is incorrect simply because it differs from your expectation.

If you believe an answer is incorrect, check the question, the officially provided response and the relevant academic evidence before deciding whether to submit an objection.

How does the NTA answer-key objection process work?

NTA will open a designated window for candidates to challenge answers in the provisional key. Candidates challenging an answer are expected to provide a valid justification and supporting evidence.

  1. Download or view the provisional answer key when NTA publishes it.
  2. Check your recorded responses against the provisional answers.
  3. Identify questions where you believe the published answer is incorrect.
  4. Review reliable academic material supporting your challenge.
  5. Submit the objection within the official deadline.
  6. Follow NTA's instructions regarding the required format and supporting documents.

Candidates should not wait until the final day if they intend to challenge an answer. A missed objection deadline may mean losing the opportunity to raise that challenge.

What happens after objections?

The provisional answer key is not necessarily the final answer key. After the objection period closes, the submitted challenges are examined by the competent authorities.

NTA will subsequently issue the final answer key, followed by the respective examination results according to the official process.

Can candidates calculate their probable score?

The provisional answer key can help candidates compare their recorded responses with the published answers and estimate a probable score. However, candidates should treat any self-calculated estimate as provisional until NTA publishes the final answer key and official result.

UGC NET candidates should watch for these updates

  • Official provisional answer key release
  • Recorded-response details, where provided
  • Objection window opening
  • Last date and instructions for submitting challenges
  • Final answer key
  • Official result announcement
