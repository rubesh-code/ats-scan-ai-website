ATS Resume for BCom & BBA Freshers in India

Learn how to build a clear, ATS-friendly resume for BCom and BBA fresher jobs in India using relevant business skills, internships, projects, education, certifications, keywords, and simple formatting.

BCom and BBA graduates can apply for entry-level opportunities in accounting, finance, banking, sales, business development, marketing, operations, human resources, customer-facing roles, and other business functions. For a fresher, the resume needs to show useful skills and evidence even when full-time experience is limited.

A strong fresher resume should make your education, relevant coursework, internships, projects, certifications, tools, and achievements easy to understand. It should also be tailored to the specific role rather than using one generic resume for every application.

Quick answer

For most BCom and BBA fresher applications, use a simple, text-readable resume with clear headings such as Summary, Skills, Education, Projects, Internship or Experience, Certifications, and Achievements where relevant. Match your genuine skills to the job description and explain how you used those skills through projects, internships, coursework, or practical work.

What should a BCom or BBA fresher resume contain?

A practical structure for an entry-level business resume is:

  1. Contact information: Name, phone number, professional email address, city/state, and optionally LinkedIn or a relevant portfolio.
  2. Professional summary: Two or three concise sentences describing your degree, strongest relevant skills, internship or project exposure, and target role.
  3. Skills: Include relevant business, finance, analytical, communication, sales, marketing, operations, and software skills that you genuinely possess.
  4. Education: BCom or BBA, institution, graduation year, and relevant academic achievements where useful.
  5. Projects: Academic or personal projects that demonstrate analysis, research, business understanding, financial work, marketing, operations, or other relevant skills.
  6. Internships or experience: Include internships, part-time work, volunteering, freelance work, or other relevant experience when available.
  7. Certifications: Add relevant certifications or courses that support the target role.
  8. Achievements: Include competitions, college leadership, presentations, events, academic awards, or other relevant accomplishments when they strengthen the application.

How to write the BCom or BBA resume summary

Avoid generic statements such as “seeking a challenging position in a reputed organisation.” Instead, connect your degree, relevant skills, practical exposure, and target role.

Example for a BCom fresher

BCom graduate with a foundation in accounting, financial analysis, Excel, and business reporting, with academic project experience in analysing financial statements. Seeking an entry-level finance or accounting role where I can apply analytical and problem-solving skills.

Example for a BBA fresher

BBA graduate with practical exposure to marketing research, Excel-based analysis, presentations, and business communication through academic projects and internship experience. Seeking an entry-level marketing, sales, business development, or operations role.

Which skills should BCom and BBA freshers list?

Your skills section should reflect the target job. Do not add every skill you have seen in job advertisements. Include skills you can explain and demonstrate.

For BCom and finance-related roles

  • Accounting fundamentals
  • Financial statement analysis
  • MS Excel
  • Financial reporting
  • Bookkeeping
  • Accounts receivable and payable fundamentals
  • Taxation fundamentals
  • Tally or other accounting software, if genuinely used

For BBA and business roles

  • Business communication
  • Market research
  • Sales fundamentals
  • Business development
  • Digital marketing fundamentals
  • Presentation skills
  • Customer relationship management
  • Operations fundamentals

Analytical and productivity tools

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Google Sheets
  • PowerPoint
  • Basic data analysis
  • Dashboard or BI tools, when genuinely used
  • CRM tools, when relevant to the target role

How to use projects when you have no work experience

Projects can demonstrate practical ability when you do not yet have full-time employment experience. Choose projects that relate to the role you want and explain what you actually did.

Weak project entry

Financial Analysis Project — College project.

Better project entry

Financial Statement Analysis — Excel

  • Analysed selected financial statements to compare revenue, expenses, profitability, and year-over-year trends.
  • Built an Excel workbook to organise calculations and present financial ratios.
  • Summarised findings in a presentation with recommendations based on the available data.

Use numbers only when they are truthful and supported by your actual work. Do not invent revenue, customers, growth percentages, or business results to make a fresher resume appear more impressive.

Should BCom and BBA freshers include internships?

Yes, when the internship is relevant. Describe your actual responsibilities and the tools or processes you used.

For example:

  • Assisted with maintaining financial records and preparing Excel reports under supervision.
  • Supported market research by collecting competitor and customer information.
  • Helped prepare presentations and reports for internal business reviews.

If you have no internship, use academic projects, coursework, certifications, college activities, volunteering, competitions, or other relevant evidence instead of leaving the resume empty.

How to choose ATS keywords for BCom and BBA jobs

Start with the actual job description. Look for the role title, required qualifications, technical tools, business skills, responsibilities, and other repeated requirements.

For example, a finance job may mention Excel, financial reporting, accounting, reconciliation, and analysis. If you genuinely have experience with those areas, make the relevant skills and evidence visible in your resume.

A sales or business development role may emphasise communication, lead generation, customer relationship management, presentations, negotiation, and business development. Tailor the resume around the requirements you can genuinely support.

Do not add keywords for skills you do not have simply because they appear frequently in job postings. Relevant and truthful keyword alignment is more useful than keyword stuffing.

ATS-friendly formatting for BCom and BBA resumes

  • Use clear section headings.
  • Keep the layout simple and easy to scan.
  • Use readable, professional fonts.
  • Use standard bullet points.
  • Avoid excessive graphics and decorative elements.
  • Avoid putting important information inside images.
  • Keep contact information as selectable text.
  • Use consistent dates and headings.
  • Follow the employer's requested file format.
  • Proofread the final document before applying.

ATS-friendly formatting helps improve machine readability, but it does not guarantee an interview or job offer. Employers may use different recruiting systems and screening processes.

BCom vs BBA resume: what should you emphasise?

BCom freshers

Emphasise accounting, finance, taxation fundamentals, Excel, financial analysis, reporting, bookkeeping, and relevant projects or internships when applying for finance, accounting, banking, or related roles.

BBA freshers

Emphasise business communication, marketing, sales, operations, market research, presentations, customer-facing skills, analytics, and relevant internships or projects depending on the target role.

Common mistakes to avoid

  1. Using one generic resume for every job. Tailor relevant sections to the target role.
  2. Listing too many unrelated skills. Include skills you can genuinely demonstrate.
  3. Writing projects as titles only. Explain your contribution, tools, process, and outcome when available.
  4. Using a highly decorative template. Prioritise readability and clear structure.
  5. Adding unverified achievements. Every claim should be truthful and defensible in an interview.
  6. Ignoring the job description. Align the resume with the requirements of the specific vacancy.
  7. Submitting without checking the final file. Make sure the document opens correctly and important text is selectable and readable.

BCom & BBA fresher resume checklist

  • ☐ Name and professional contact information are correct
  • ☐ Target role is clear
  • ☐ Summary is specific to the target job
  • ☐ Skills are truthful and relevant
  • ☐ Education is easy to find
  • ☐ Projects show useful business or analytical work
  • ☐ Internship experience is included when relevant
  • ☐ Relevant certifications are included
  • ☐ Job-description keywords are used naturally
  • ☐ Formatting is simple and readable
  • ☐ Resume has been checked for spelling and grammar
  • ☐ Final file follows the employer's requested format

Check your resume before applying

Before sending your BCom or BBA fresher resume, check how well it matches the target job description and whether the important information is readable and clearly organised.

Final thoughts

A strong BCom or BBA fresher resume does not need to list every subject, software tool, or generic soft skill. It should make your strongest and most relevant evidence easy to understand.

Start with the target job description, choose skills you can genuinely support, explain your projects clearly, highlight relevant internships and certifications, and keep the document simple and readable. Then review the finished resume before submitting it.
