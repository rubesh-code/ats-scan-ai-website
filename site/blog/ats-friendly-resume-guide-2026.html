This guide explains what an ATS-friendly resume is, how to structure it correctly, how to improve its relevance to a job description, and how to avoid common mistakes.

What Is an ATS-Friendly Resume?

An ATS-friendly resume is designed so that an Applicant Tracking System can correctly read, organise, and understand its content.

An ATS may scan information such as:

  • Contact details
  • Work experience
  • Education
  • Technical and professional skills
  • Job titles
  • Certifications
  • Keywords related to the job description

A resume may look impressive to a person but still perform poorly in an ATS if it uses a complicated layout, unreadable graphics, or lacks relevant job keywords.

Why Is an ATS Resume Important?

When you submit a job application online, your resume may be processed by software before it reaches a recruiter.

An ATS-friendly resume can help you:

  • Present your qualifications clearly
  • Match your resume with the job description
  • Avoid formatting and parsing errors
  • Highlight relevant skills
  • Make your application easier to review
An ATS-friendly format does not guarantee an interview. Your experience, skills, and qualifications must still match the employer’s requirements.

How to Create an ATS-Friendly Resume

1. Use a Simple Resume Format

Choose a clean, professional, and easy-to-read layout. A reverse-chronological format is suitable for many applicants because it presents the most recent work experience first.

Your resume should generally include:

  1. Contact information
  2. Professional summary
  3. Key skills
  4. Work experience
  5. Education
  6. Certifications
  7. Projects or achievements

2. Add the Correct Job Keywords

Read the job description carefully and identify recurring terms related to required skills, software, qualifications, responsibilities, industry knowledge, and experience.

Example keywords for a digital marketing role: Search engine optimisation, Google Analytics, content marketing, social media campaigns, keyword research, and lead generation.

Include relevant terms naturally in your professional summary, skills, projects, and experience. Do not copy the complete job description or add skills you do not possess.

3. Use Standard Section Headings

Recommended headings include:

  • Professional Summary
  • Skills
  • Work Experience
  • Education
  • Certifications
  • Projects
  • Achievements

Avoid creative headings such as “My Journey” or “What I Know,” because an ATS may not interpret them as accurately as standard headings.

4. Write a Relevant Professional Summary

Your professional summary should briefly explain your experience, specialisation, strongest skills, and career focus.

Example: Results-driven digital marketing specialist with four years of experience in SEO, content marketing, Google Analytics, and paid advertising. Experienced in increasing organic traffic and managing performance-focused campaigns.

5. Add a Focused Skills Section

Create a dedicated skills section containing the technical and professional skills most relevant to the target position.

Example for a software developer: JavaScript, React, Node.js, REST APIs, Git, MySQL, AWS, and Agile development.

6. Describe Achievements, Not Only Responsibilities

Strong resume bullet points explain what you achieved rather than only listing routine duties.

Weak: Responsible for managing social media accounts.

Better: Managed content across three social media platforms and increased average monthly engagement by 35% within six months.

7. Use Clear Job Titles and Dates

Use a consistent format for each position:

Frontend Developer ABC Technologies | Bengaluru, India
January 2023–Present

8. Choose ATS-Friendly Fonts

Use professional fonts such as:

  • Arial
  • Calibri
  • Helvetica
  • Georgia
  • Times New Roman
  • Verdana

9. Avoid Complicated Design Elements

An ATS may have difficulty reading:

  • Complex tables
  • Text boxes
  • Multiple columns
  • Charts and skill-rating bars
  • Icons used instead of words
  • Images containing important text
  • Decorative graphics

10. Save the Resume in the Correct File Format

Follow the file-format instructions in the job advertisement. DOCX is commonly accepted. A text-based PDF can also work when permitted, but avoid scanned or image-based PDFs.

Use a professional file name such as Rahul-Sharma-Software-Developer-Resume.pdf.

How to Make an ATS Resume for Freshers

Freshers may not have extensive professional experience, but they can still create a strong ATS-friendly resume by highlighting:

  • Education
  • Internships
  • Academic projects
  • Freelance work
  • Volunteer experience
  • Technical skills
  • Certifications
  • Relevant achievements
Example project bullet: Developed a React-based expense-tracking application with user authentication, category-based reports, and responsive mobile layouts.

How Does an ATS Resume Checker Work?

An ATS resume checker may analyse your resume and compare it with a job description. Depending on the tool, it may review:

  • Keyword relevance
  • Skills matching
  • Section headings
  • Contact details
  • Resume length
  • Formatting
  • Measurable achievements
  • Missing information
  • Readability
  • File compatibility

Treat an ATS score as guidance rather than a guaranteed prediction of an employer’s decision. Different resume checkers may use different scoring methods.

Common ATS Resume Mistakes

Using One Resume for Every Job

Every role has different requirements. Update your summary, skills, and experience based on the target job.

Adding Keywords Without Context

A long list of keywords does not prove experience. Connect important skills to real achievements or projects whenever possible.

Using an Overdesigned Template

Heavy graphics, multiple columns, and skill bars may interfere with resume parsing.

Hiding Important Information in Images

An ATS may not correctly read text embedded in logos, screenshots, or graphical elements.

Adding Incorrect or Misleading Skills

Include only experience and skills you can confidently explain during an interview.

Ignoring Spelling and Grammar

Proofread names, dates, contact information, job titles, and technical terms before submitting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the full form of ATS in a resume?

ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. It is software that helps employers collect, organise, search, and review job applications.

What is an ATS resume?

An ATS resume is formatted and written so that recruitment software can correctly read its sections, skills, experience, education, and qualifications.

Which resume format is best for ATS?

A clean reverse-chronological resume with standard headings and a simple layout is suitable for many candidates.

Is PDF or Word better for ATS?

Use the format requested by the employer. DOCX is commonly accepted, while a text-based PDF may also work. Avoid scanned or image-based files.

What is a good ATS resume score?

There is no universal passing score. Use the result to identify missing keywords and formatting issues rather than treating it as a guaranteed hiring outcome.

Can freshers create an ATS-friendly resume?

Yes. Freshers can focus on education, projects, internships, certifications, technical skills, volunteer work, and measurable achievements.
