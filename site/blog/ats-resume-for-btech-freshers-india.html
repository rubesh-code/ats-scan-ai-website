ATS Resume for BTech Freshers in India (2026): Skills, Projects & Format

A practical guide to building a focused BTech fresher resume for software, data, engineering, cloud, and other entry-level roles in India.

ATS Scan AI Editorial TeamUpdated 8 August 202610 min read
Quick answer

A strong BTech fresher resume is usually one page, single-column, text-based, and tailored to the target role. Put relevant skills, projects, internships, education, certifications, and measurable evidence where a recruiter can find them quickly.

What should a BTech fresher resume contain?

A useful structure is:

  1. Name and professional contact details
  2. Two- or three-line professional summary
  3. Relevant technical skills
  4. Projects
  5. Internships or practical experience
  6. Education
  7. Certifications and relevant achievements

Move projects above education when your practical work is stronger evidence for the target role. For campus placements, education may deserve more prominence.

1. Write a role-specific summary

Your summary should identify the role you want and the skills or evidence that support it. Avoid a generic objective that could belong to any graduate.

Example: Software Engineer Fresher

Computer Science BTech graduate targeting entry-level software engineering roles, with hands-on experience in Java, Python, SQL, Git, REST APIs, and React through academic and personal projects.

For data roles, emphasize SQL, Excel, Power BI, Python, statistics, dashboards, and data projects. Tailoring the summary is more useful than listing every technology you have touched.

2. Choose skills based on the job

There is no single list of “best BTech skills.” Match your skills to the target vacancy and only include tools you can discuss confidently.

Software development

Depending on the role: Java, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, SQL, REST APIs, Git, testing, and data structures and algorithms.

Data analyst roles

Depending on the role: SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Python, Pandas, data cleaning, statistics, dashboards, and reporting.

Cloud or DevOps roles

Relevant evidence may include Linux, Git, Docker, CI/CD, AWS, Azure, networking fundamentals, scripting, and infrastructure tools when they genuinely match your experience.

3. Make projects the proof behind your skills

Projects can demonstrate that a skill is more than a keyword. Describe your contribution, technologies, and result.

Weak

Built a web application using React and Node.js.

Stronger

Built a React and Node.js task-management application with REST API integration, authentication, and responsive screens; deployed it for portfolio testing.

Use numbers when they are real: records processed, response time reduced, users tested, features delivered, or test cases written. Never invent metrics.

4. Include internships and practical experience

If you completed an internship, describe what you actually did rather than only listing the company and title. Explain the tools used, the problem worked on, and what you delivered.

For a short internship, two or three strong bullets are better than a paragraph of responsibilities. If an internship is unrelated to the target role, keep it concise and give more space to relevant projects.

5. Present BTech education clearly

List your degree, specialization, college or university, location, and graduation year. Include CGPA or percentage when useful or requested.

Example

B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering
ABC Institute of Technology, Pune, Maharashtra
2022–2026 · CGPA: 8.3/10

Add relevant coursework only when it supports the target role, such as database systems, operating systems, computer networks, or algorithms.

6. Keep the Indian application context simple

Your base resume may be used for company career portals, job boards, campus placement systems, LinkedIn, and recruiter outreach. Keep one clean master resume and tailor the content for each role.

Unless specifically requested, avoid a photograph, date of birth, marital status, religion, Aadhaar number, or other unnecessary personal information. Keep the document focused on qualifications and evidence.

7. Use ATS-friendly formatting

  • Use a single-column layout as a safe default.
  • Use standard headings such as Summary, Skills, Education, Projects, and Experience.
  • Keep dates and job titles consistent.
  • Use readable fonts and normal text.
  • Avoid putting important information inside images, text boxes, or decorative graphics.
  • Follow the employer’s requested file format.
  • Use a text-based PDF or DOCX rather than an image-only scan.

For more detail, read ATS Resume Format for Freshers in India and PDF vs DOCX for ATS.

8. Tailor your resume to each job description

Identify the job title, required skills, responsibilities, qualifications, and important domain terms. Reflect the relevant ones in your summary, skills, projects, and experience where they are accurate.

See How to Find Resume Keywords in a Job Description for a detailed method. Do not copy the entire job description into your resume or add unsupported keywords.

9. What BTech freshers should remove

  • Generic objective statements
  • Long lists of unrelated tools
  • Unsupported claims such as “expert” without evidence
  • Long paragraphs about every college activity
  • Unnecessary personal information
  • Decorative graphics that replace text
  • Duplicate skills repeated in every section

10. Final BTech fresher ATS checklist

  • ☐ Target role is obvious within the first few lines.
  • ☐ Skills match the target job and are truthful.
  • ☐ Projects show what you built or contributed.
  • ☐ Internship bullets describe actual work.
  • ☐ Education includes degree, institution, and dates.
  • ☐ No unnecessary personal identifiers.
  • ☐ Important information is not hidden in graphics or text boxes.
  • ☐ File format follows the employer’s instructions.
  • ☐ Resume has been checked against the target job description.
  • ☐ Claims and metrics are accurate.
