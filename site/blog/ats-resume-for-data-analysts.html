ATS Resume Guide for Data Analysts

A data analyst resume is strongest when it connects tools and analysis methods to decisions, efficiency, and measurable business outcomes.

ATS Scan AI Editorial TeamUpdated 5 August 20269 min read
Key point

A data analyst resume is strongest when it connects tools and analysis methods to decisions, efficiency, and measurable business outcomes.

Show the full analysis workflow

Describe how you collected, cleaned, validated, analyzed, and presented data. This gives more context than listing SQL, Excel, or Power BI alone.

Prove SQL and spreadsheet use

Mention the type of queries, transformations, models, or automation you created. For spreadsheets, show the reporting or operational problem solved.

Explain dashboard impact

A dashboard is valuable when it changes a decision or reduces manual work. Include the audience, refresh process, metrics, and outcome where possible.

Include data-quality work

Validation rules, reconciliations, missing-data handling, source checks, and documentation demonstrate trustworthy analysis.

Connect insights to decisions

Strong bullets explain what the analysis revealed and what action followed. Avoid claiming business impact when the connection cannot be supported.

Tailor by domain

Finance, marketing, operations, healthcare, and product analytics use different metrics and language. Reflect the target domain when your experience supports it.
